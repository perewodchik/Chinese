# 走走 — the real Beijing in the game

Every real place you can visit or see in 走走, with what makes it worth
knowing. For reading, not for building: a fact that a game line, book or
place card uses must first get a row in `facts.md` with its source. Facts
already checked there are marked ✓. The rest is well-known history. Treat it
as "probably right" until it has a row.

Ordered by chapter. Each place has: what it is, the cool part, what the game
does there, and how to get there for real.

## Chapter 1 新家 — 鼓楼 · 南锣鼓巷 (and 天安门 at the end)

### 胡同 hútòng and 四合院 sìhéyuàn — the lanes and courtyard houses
- **What:** 胡同 are the narrow lanes of old Beijing. A 四合院 is a house of four buildings around a courtyard, closed to the street.
- **Cool:**
  - The house faces south. The north rooms, which get the sun, are for the oldest people in the family.
  - A screen wall 影壁 stands inside the gate so that no one sees straight in. People also say it keeps spirits out, because they can only move in straight lines.
  - The stone drums or blocks by a gate are 门墩. People say their shape showed what the owner did.
  - The word 胡同 may come from a Mongolian word for "well", since the lanes go back to the Yuan dynasty. That's a theory, not a certainty.
- **In the game:** you live in 王阿姨's 四合院 on 帽儿胡同 (your room, the yard, the old lantern, the 槐树).
- **For real:** 帽儿胡同 is off 南锣鼓巷 (line 6/8, 南锣鼓巷 station).

### 南锣鼓巷 Nánluógǔxiàng — the centipede street
- **What:** a 786 m lane running north–south, about 740 years old (Yuan dynasty city plan). ✓
- **Cool:**
  - It has **16 胡同 branching off, 8 on each side, like the legs of a centipede**, so people call it 蜈蚣街. ✓ West, north to south: 前鼓楼苑, 黑芝麻, 沙井, 景阳, 帽儿, 雨儿, 蓑衣, 福祥. East: 菊儿, 后圆恩寺, 前圆恩寺, 秦老, 北兵马司, 东棉花, 板厂, 炒豆. ✓
  - The name: the lane is higher in the middle, like a hunched back (罗锅), so it was called 罗锅巷, and that became 锣鼓巷.
  - Famous people lived in its side lanes:
    - the painter **齐白石**, famous for shrimp (雨儿胡同);
    - the writer **茅盾** (后圆恩寺胡同);
    - **婉容**, the last empress, grew up in 帽儿胡同, which also has the small classical garden 可园;
    - the **中央戏剧学院** (Central Academy of Drama), where China's actors study, is on 东棉花胡同.
  - **菊儿胡同** has new courtyard houses designed by the architect 吴良镛 around 1990. They won a UN World Habitat Award for showing how old lanes can be rebuilt without being torn down.
- **In the game:** the 早点铺, 小卖部, 理发店, 茶馆; 老马's 煎饼 and 米沙's 豹子. The 16 lane names are signed, but today their mouths lead nowhere. See STATUS, MH2.
- **For real:** line 6/8 南锣鼓巷 station at its south end. ✓ Very crowded at weekends; the side lanes are quiet.

### 鼓楼 Gǔlóu and 钟楼 Zhōnglóu — the Drum and Bell Towers
- **What:** the towers that kept the city's time, at the north end of Beijing's central axis. The 鼓楼 is south, the 钟楼 north, with a square between. ✓
- **Cool:**
  - 晨钟暮鼓: the bell and the drum marked the five night watches. ✓
  - At about 7 in the evening (定更) the drum sounded first, then the bell, 108 strokes each time ("紧十八，慢十八，不紧不慢又十八", twice), and the city gates shut. ✓
  - The 鼓楼 had **25 drums**: one big one for the year and 24 for the 24 节气 (solar terms). ✓
  - The 钟楼's bronze bell, cast under the 永乐 emperor, is about 7 m tall and weighs 63 tonnes. It is the heaviest old bronze bell in China. ✓
  - Drummers still play for visitors every hour or so in the day. ✓
  - The 钟楼 was rebuilt in brick and stone in the 1740s after a fire, so that it could not burn again.
- **In the game:** book 3 《晨钟暮鼓》; listen for the drum at dusk.
- **For real:** walk north up 南锣鼓巷 and turn west on 鼓楼东大街, or take line 8 to 什刹海.

### 天安门 Tiān'ānmén — the Gate of Heavenly Peace
- **What:** the front gate of the old imperial city, south of the Forbidden City.
- **Cool:**
  - It was built in 1417 as 承天门 and rebuilt in 1651 under the new Qing dynasty, which renamed it 天安门.
  - Five arches go through the red wall. The middle one was for the emperor alone.
  - Five marble bridges cross the 金水河 in front, one for each arch. The middle bridge was the emperor's too.
  - The two carved stone columns in front are **华表**. Each has a small beast, a 犼, on top. A folk story says the pair facing south watch for the emperor to come home ("望君归"), and the pair behind the gate watch him so he doesn't stay away too long ("望君出").
  - On 1 October 1949 the People's Republic was proclaimed from the gate tower.
  - 天安门广场, the square in front, is one of the largest city squares in the world. Around it stand:
    - the 人民英雄纪念碑 (Monument to the People's Heroes) in the middle;
    - the 人民大会堂 (Great Hall of the People) to the west;
    - the 国家博物馆 (National Museum) to the east;
    - 正阳门 (前门) to the south.
  - **The flag is raised every morning at sunrise** by a guard of honour that marches out of the gate. People come before dawn to see it.
- **In the game:** the end of chapter 1, after a long subway ride, but today there is almost nothing there (one line, a guard and a photographer). See STATUS, RW3.
- **For real:** line 1, 天安门东 or 天安门西. You need your passport and a security check; the square needs a free booking in advance.

## Chapter 2 水与山 — 什刹海 · 北海 · 景山

### 烟袋斜街 Yāndài Xiéjiē — Tobacco Pipe Lane
- **What:** a short slanting lane from the 鼓楼 to the lakes, one of Beijing's oldest shopping streets.
- **Cool:** it is shaped like a long tobacco pipe: the straight part is the stem, the bend at the lake end is the bowl. In the Qing it was full of shops selling pipes.
- **In the game:** the way from 鼓楼 to 银锭桥, with a glimpse of it at the end of chapter 1.

### 银锭桥 Yíndìng Qiáo and 什刹海 Shíchàhǎi — the lakes
- **What:** 什刹海 is three lakes in a row: 前海, 后海 and 西海. They are over 700 years old. ✓ The little stone bridge 银锭桥, shaped like a silver ingot, crosses the neck between 前海 and 后海. ✓
- **Cool:**
  - 银锭观山: on a clear day you can see the Western Hills from the bridge. It is one of the 燕京小八景, the "little eight views" of old Beijing. ✓
  - The name 什刹海 is said to come from ten (什) Buddhist temples (刹) that once stood around it.
  - In winter the lakes freeze and become a huge skating rink, with ice bikes and chair sleds. Old Beijing skated there too. ✓
- **In the game:** the fisherman, the view cutscene.
- **For real:** line 8, 什刹海 station.

### 恭王府 Gōngwángfǔ — Prince Gong's Mansion
- **What:** the biggest surviving prince's mansion in Beijing, with a large garden, by 后海.
- **Cool:**
  - Before Prince Gong it belonged to **和珅**, the 乾隆 emperor's favourite minister and one of the richest (and most corrupt) men in Chinese history.
  - Hidden in a rockery in the garden is a stone with the character **福** written by the 康熙 emperor, called "the first 福 under heaven". People queue to touch it for luck.
- **In the game:** its gate on the chapter 2 route. (`facts.md` still has the garden as `?`.)

### 北海 Běihǎi and the White Dagoba 白塔
- **What:** one of the oldest imperial gardens in China, nearly a thousand years old, around a lake just north-west of the palace.
- **Cool:**
  - The 白塔 on the island 琼华岛 is a Tibetan-style dagoba, 35.9 m tall, built in 1651, a year before the Fifth Dalai Lama visited Beijing. ✓
  - The park has its own **九龙壁**, a Nine-Dragon Screen with dragons on both sides.
  - The song 《让我们荡起双桨》, which every Chinese child knows, is about rowing a boat on 北海.
- **In the game:** book 4; boats from the north shore. ✓

### 景山 Jǐngshān — Coal Hill
- **What:** a green hill right behind the Forbidden City.
- **Cool:**
  - The hill is man-made, from the earth dug out for the palace moat.
  - Five pavilions line its ridge. The middle one, **万春亭**, is the highest and sits exactly on the city's central axis. ✓
  - From it you see the whole palace, golden roof after golden roof: the best view in Beijing.
  - In 1644, as rebels took the city, the last Ming emperor 崇祯 died on this hill. A tree marks the place.
- **In the game:** 甜甜's livestream (「不对，是明朝。」), the view cutscene.

### 角楼 Jiǎolóu — the palace corner towers
- **What:** the four towers at the corners of the palace wall, mirrored in the moat.
- **Cool:**
  - Each one has "nine beams, eighteen pillars and seventy-two ridges" (九梁十八柱七十二条脊).
  - A legend says the carpenters couldn't design it until the master carpenter 鲁班 appeared as an old man selling a cricket cage of exactly that shape.
  - The moat around the palace is 52 m wide.
- **In the game:** the white fox at night (狐假虎威, book 5).

## Chapter 3 书 — 王府井 · 前门

### 王府井 Wángfǔjǐng
- **What:** Beijing's best-known shopping street.
- **Cool:**
  - The name means "princes' mansions well". There really was a well with sweet water, and a bronze cover in the pavement marks the spot.
  - 百货大楼 (1955) was called "new China's first shop". A bronze statue outside shows 张秉贵, a famous clerk who could grab exactly the weight of sweets a customer asked for in one go (一抓准).
  - 盛锡福 has made hats since 1911.
  - The snack street sells scorpions on sticks, mostly to tourists. Locals eat the 糖葫芦.
- **In the game:** the bank, 百货大楼 (gift for 王阿姨), 新华书店 (books), 药店 (兔儿爷's cold), 老牛's 煎饼.
- **For real:** line 1, 王府井 station.

### 前门 Qiánmén and 正阳门 Zhèngyángmén
- **What:** the great south gate of the old inner city (正阳门, 1419) and its arrow tower 箭楼, with 前门大街 running south.
- **Cool:**
  - The gates sit on the line of the old city wall. **Subway line 2 runs in a loop where the wall used to be**, and many of its stations are named after the old gates: 前门, 崇文门, 朝阳门, 东直门, 安定门, 德胜门, 西直门, 宣武门.
  - The little 铛铛车 tram on 前门大街 copies Beijing's old trams. It is named after its bell.
  - 全聚德 has roasted duck here since 1864.
- **In the game:** the opera house (京剧, 脸谱), the 门神 who must face each other. ✓

### 大栅栏 Dàzhàlan — "say it Dàshílànr"
- **What:** an old shopping lane full of time-honoured shops (老字号).
- **Cool:**
  - The name means "big wooden fence". In the Qing, lanes had gates that were shut at night.
  - Beijingers say **Dàshílànr**, not the textbook Dàzhàlán. It's a good test of whether you know the city.
  - An old saying lists what a rich man wore: 「头戴马聚源，身穿瑞蚨祥，脚踩内联升」 (a hat from 马聚源, silk from 瑞蚨祥, shoes from 内联升).
  - **瑞蚨祥** (silk, here since 1893) supplied the cloth for the first national flag raised on 1 October 1949.
  - **内联升** has made cloth-soled shoes since 1853. It made shoes for officials at court.
- **In the game:** a proper outfit for opera night at 瑞蚨祥, shoes at 内联升, the granny with 英子's bell.

## Chapter 4 回声 — 天坛 · 国子监 · 孔庙 · 雍和宫

### 天坛 Tiāntán — the Temple of Heaven
- **What:** where the emperor, the "Son of Heaven", prayed to heaven: for a good harvest in spring at the 祈年殿, and to heaven itself at the 圜丘 at the winter solstice. ✓
- **Cool:**
  - 天圆地方: its buildings are round and roofed in blue (sky-coloured) tiles, and the outer wall is round in the north and square in the south. ✓
  - The **祈年殿** stands on 28 pillars, people say: 4 for the seasons, 12 for the months and 12 for the hours of the old day.
  - The 祈年殿 burned in 1889 after lightning struck it and was rebuilt to the old design by 1896. ✓
  - **回音壁**, the Echo Wall: whisper into the round wall and a friend far round the other side hears you.
  - **圜丘**: stand on the centre stone 天心石 and your voice comes back from all round, louder. ✓ Its stones and steps all come in multiples of nine, the emperor's number.
  - 丹陛桥, a raised walkway, links the two halves.
  - Today the park belongs to locals: morning tai chi, singing, 毽子 (shuttlecock kicking), chess, 胡琴.
- **In the game:** book 8, 小明's school trip, the echo beat.
- **For real:** line 5, 天坛东门.

### 国子监 Guózǐjiān and 孔庙 Kǒngmiào
- **What:** the highest school of the Yuan, Ming and Qing (from 1287), and the Confucius Temple beside it. ✓
- **Cool:**
  - 国子监街 is the only street in Beijing that still has its 牌楼 archways (four). ✓
  - The emperor came to lecture in the round hall 辟雍, which sits in a round pool.
  - The 孔庙 has 198 stone steles carved with the names of **51,624 进士**, the scholars who passed the highest imperial exam. ✓ The exams ended in 1905. ✓
  - 「三人行，必有我师焉」 (论语): of any three people walking together, one can teach me something. ✓
- **In the game:** book 9 《科举》, the 麒麟 at dusk.
- **For real:** line 2/5, 雍和宫 station, then walk west.

### 雍和宫 Yōnghégōng — the Lama Temple
- **What:** a Tibetan Buddhist temple that was once a prince's mansion. The prince became the 雍正 emperor, and the house became a temple in 1744. ✓
- **Cool:**
  - The Maitreya Buddha in 万福阁 is carved from **one** white sandalwood trunk: 18 m above the ground and 8 m below. It is in the Guinness book. ✓
  - Visitors light three sticks of incense and bow. It is a working temple, so be quiet.
- **In the game:** 胡半仙 tells you you're a rabbit; chapter 5 comes back here.

### 地坛 Dìtán — the Altar of Earth
- **What:** heaven's partner. Earth was square, so its altar is square (天圆地方 again).
- **Cool:** it holds one of Beijing's biggest 春节 temple fairs (庙会), with snacks, performers and games. ✓

## Chapter 5 香火 — 白云观 · 东岳庙 (built later)

### 白云观 Báiyúnguàn — the White Cloud Temple
- **What:** the centre of the 全真 school of Daoism, first founded in the Tang dynasty.
- **Cool:**
  - Three small stone monkeys are carved in different places, and you have to hunt for them ("三猴不见面"). Touching one is said to bring luck, and the one at the gate is rubbed smooth. ✓
  - Under the bridge 窝风桥 hangs a big wooden coin with a bell in its hole. At 春节 people throw coins to ring it (打金钱眼). ✓
- **For real:** line 1, 木樨地, then about a 1 km walk. ✓

### 东岳庙 Dōngyuèmiào
- **What:** a Daoist temple from 1319 near 朝阳门. ✓
- **Cool:** its side halls hold the "departments" of the afterlife, traditionally 72 but really 76, each with its own judge. ✓ Today it is the Beijing Folk Custom Museum. ✓

## Chapter 6 新北京 — 三里屯 · 国贸 · 奥林匹克 (built later)

### 三里屯 Sānlǐtún
- **What:** embassies, bars and the 太古里 shopping streets.
- **Cool:** the name means "village three 里 away", three 里 (about 1.5 km) from the old city wall at 东直门.

### 国贸 Guómào — the business district
- **What:** Beijing's CBD of glass towers.
- **Cool:** the tallest building in Beijing, the 528 m 中国尊 (CITIC Tower), is shaped like a 尊, an ancient bronze wine vessel.
- **In the game:** the golden 貔貅 in the bank, a beast that "eats gold and never lets it out", which is why banks and shops like it.

### 奥林匹克公园 — 鸟巢 Niǎocháo and 水立方 Shuǐlìfāng
- **What:** the park of the 2008 Olympics.
- **Cool:**
  - Beijing is the **first city to host both the Summer (2008) and the Winter (2022) Olympics**. The Bird's Nest held the opening ceremony both times.
  - The Water Cube was turned into the "Ice Cube" 冰立方 for curling in 2022.
- **For real:** line 8, 奥林匹克公园.

## Chapter 7 故事 — 颐和园 · 潘家园 (built later)

### 颐和园 Yíhéyuán — the Summer Palace
- **What:** the Qing emperors' huge summer garden around 昆明湖 in the north-west.
- **Cool:**
  - 乾隆 built it (as 清漪园) for his mother's birthday. It was burned in 1860, and the Empress Dowager 慈禧 rebuilt it in the 1880s, people say partly with money meant for the navy.
  - The **长廊**, the Long Corridor, is 728 m long with more than 14,000 paintings, many of them scenes from the four classic novels. ✓
  - The **十七孔桥** (Seventeen-Arch Bridge): for a few days around the winter solstice the setting sun shines through all 17 arches at once, called 金光穿洞. Crowds wait hours for it.
  - A marble boat 石舫 that can never sail.
- **For real:** line 4, 北宫门; the game takes bus 332 from 西直门. ✓

### 潘家园 Pānjiāyuán — the antiques market
- **What:** China's biggest antiques and flea market: jade, coins, old photos, posters, Mao badges, and plenty of fakes.
- **Cool:** it began as a dawn "ghost market" (鬼市) where people traded by torchlight. Go early at the weekend, and **bargain** (砍价).
- **In the game:** the 年兽, bargaining, and later 米沙 selling his watch by mistake.

## Chapter 8 龙 — 故宫 (the Forbidden City)

### 故宫 Gùgōng / 紫禁城 Zǐjìnchéng
- **What:** the palace of 24 emperors (14 Ming, 10 Qing), built 1406–1420 under the 永乐 emperor. ✓ It has been a museum since 1925.
- **Cool:**
  - The legend says it has 9,999½ rooms (only heaven may have 10,000). A 1973 survey counted 8,704. (`?` in facts.md)
  - **午门**, the Meridian Gate, is where you go in. ✓
  - **太和殿**, the Hall of Supreme Harmony, is China's largest old wooden hall, used for coronations and the New Year. It has **10 little beasts** on each roof ridge, more than any other building. The tenth, 行什, appears only here. ✓
  - **乾清宫** was the emperor's residence. From 雍正 on, the name of the next emperor was hidden in a box behind the plaque 「正大光明」, so no one knew it until the old emperor died.
  - **Door studs (门钉):** the main gates have 9 × 9 = 81. **东华门 has only 72.** People say it was the gate for funerals. ✓ (reason ~)
  - **九龙壁:** nine glazed-tile dragons. Legend says a worker broke one tile and secretly carved a wooden one, painted to match, to save his life. It is still there.
  - **御花园**, the Imperial Garden, at the north end; you leave by **神武门**.
- **In the game:** the ticket in your name, 画龙点睛 as the biggest cutscene.
- **For real:** book online days ahead with your passport; line 1, 天安门东.

## Chapter 9 过年 and 尾声 — 长城 (built later)

### 长城 Chángchéng at 八达岭 Bādálǐng
- **What:** the most visited part of the Great Wall, a Ming wall climbing a steep ridge.
- **Cool:**
  - 「不到长城非好汉」: you're not a real hero until you've been to the Wall.
  - The first railway here, **京张铁路 (1909)**, was the first major railway in China designed by a Chinese engineer, 詹天佑. To climb the mountains he laid the track in a zig-zag (人字形) at 青龙桥.
  - The new high-speed line (2019) has its 八达岭长城 station about 100 m under the mountain, one of the deepest in the world.
- **In the game:** the 京张高铁 from 北京北站, the spirits' farewell.

## Getting around: the Beijing subway (地铁 dìtiě)
- Line 1 opened in **1969**, the first subway in China. Today the system is one of the longest and busiest in the world.
- **Line 2 is a loop on the line of the old city wall** (see 前门). It runs both ways: 内环 (inner loop) and 外环 (outer loop).
- Trains say 「下一站是……」, 「列车即将到达……站」, 「换乘……的乘客，请在……站下车」, 「请站稳扶好」. ✓
- Most stations have platform screen doors, and a screen above each one counts down the next two trains for that direction.
- No bicycles, not even folding ones. ✓ Every station has a bag scanner at the entrance.
