# S3 — chapter 3 书, deepened (design note)

**Why.** Chapter 3 was six steps: arrive, bank, 成语 book, opera house, red paper,
door gods. 王府井's department store, pharmacy and the 大栅栏 shops were only
side quests or scenery. Now it has 12 steps, and each one is an everyday Chinese
act the learner will really need:
- buying a present (「送给谁？什么颜色？」);
- describing symptoms at a pharmacy (发烧, 咳嗽 — the two words you most need
  there, with the classic answer 「多喝热水！」);
- accepting an invitation;
- borrowing clothes;
- answering from a book at the opera;
- telling the door gods how they must stand.

**What the player sees.** Opening over 王府井. The bank. A red scarf for 王阿姨 at
百货大楼. The 成语 book. The pharmacy for 兔儿爷's cold ("Hot water? I'm made of
clay!"). At the 前门 opera house the actor says the door gods have lost their
colour, and the owner invites you to tonight's show and gives 《脸谱》. 瑞蚨祥
lends a 唐装 for the evening. At the show the owner asks what colour 关羽's face is
(book p. 3); then a short 京剧 cutscene: 关羽 steps out to the gong and drums. The
bookshop sells red paper and gives 《门神》. At the gate the door gods ask how
they should stand: 面对面 (book p. 6). Then 画蛇添足, as before. Home, 王阿姨
gets her scarf and remembers her father painting the door gods (memory 3).

**Data.** New `ch3` steps gift · cold · lianpu-book · outfit · show · home, with
the old ids kept in their order. `c3-*` scenes in wangfujing and qianmen, items
`weijin` and `tangzhuang` with sprites, books `menshen` and `lianpu`, cutscenes
`ch3-open` (wangfujing), `c3-jingju`, `memory-3`, `ch3-finale` (qianmen), and
facts `menshen-generals`, `menshen-face`, `lianpu-colours` (now ✓).

**Edge cases.**
- The door gods now take the red paper only at the `menshen` step. The old
  scene waits for every step before it, and the new one asks for the step.
  Before, they could be woken on the first visit to 前门.
- The show needs the lent jacket.
- The scarf costs 50 元, after the bank has given 700.
- The golden save `mid-ch3.json` (at `book`, made with the content before S3)
  keeps its step and plays to the end.

**Not done.** The 京剧 cutscene uses the actor's normal sprite (no red face
paint yet); 兔儿爷 names the colour. The 铛铛车 on 前门大街 is not a step.
