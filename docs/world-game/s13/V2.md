# V2 — the Beijing architecture kit

**Why.** The world looked flat: roofs were grey bands, every building the
same. Real Beijing tells its story in its roofs — who may use which colour
is itself a lesson (book 《故宫》) — and a learner who recognises a green
temple roof or the emperor's yellow has learned something true.

**What exists now** (`scripts/world/art/gen/kit.ts`, `gen/kit-props.ts`;
review sheet `docs/world-game/review/v/kit.png`):
- **Roofs in the four glazes of the hierarchy** — grey 筒瓦 (houses,
  硬山 gable ends with a 蝎子尾 curl), green (princes and temples), yellow
  (the emperor, with 脊兽 dots down the hip ridge), blue (heaven, the 祈年殿).
  Twelve tiles each: ridge 正脊 (with a 正吻 at a hip roof's ends), upper
  slope (steeper: courses closer, a shade darker), lower slope, eave (round
  瓦当 tile ends, the overhang's dark edge, a flying corner 翼角 with its
  bell) — plain, left end, right end. Hip ends cut the slope with the hip
  ridge, so a hall is a trapezoid with its pitch visible, not a band. A 重檐
  double roof is a small roof over a painted band over a wide one.
- **Faces under the eaves** (their top rows in the eave's shadow): 旋子彩画
  blue-green beams, 苏式 beams with a little picture (the Long Corridor), red
  wall, red lattice doors 隔扇, a red pillar on its stone base, palace door
  leaves with 门钉 (two make a door), a carved 丹陛 ramp.
- **North–south streets:** a hutong wall and a house roof seen along a
  street running north–south (coping, the shaded east face or the lit west
  face, ridge beam, the eave along the street) — so 南锣鼓巷, 王府井 and
  前门大街 can run the way they really do (T1, T4).
- **Props (23):** 香炉 (smoking, 2 frames, and cold), 转经筒 (3 frames), 石碑
  on 赑屃, a temple bell, 蒲团, 签筒, 影壁, 垂花门, a glazed 牌坊, a lioness
  with her cub (the lion of the pair already holds its ball); overhead wires,
  an AC box, a washing line, 大白菜, 煤球, 春联 with an upside-down 福, 小广告,
  a 公厕 sign, a 烤串 grill (smoke, 2 frames), a 冰糖葫芦 stand, a 修车摊, a
  newspaper kiosk, a 共享单车 rack. Existing props (towers, 白塔, 祈年殿,
  pavilion, lion, steamers, bicycles, 三轮车) and V1's lamps, benches and
  trees complete the set.
- **Maps may add their own letters** for kit tiles in their header
  (`below_keys: 1=roof-green-ridge-l 2=roof-green-ridge …`), since the kit
  has more tiles than the shared legend has letters.

**Not done here.** Placing the kit on maps (T1–T4, V5); 金水桥 as a bridge
piece (T4 draws it with the palace); separate 攒尖 and triple-round roof
modules — those buildings stay whole props (万春亭, 祈年殿). Real 门钉 rows
are nine by nine; a 16-px tile shows the pattern, and the book says the
number.
