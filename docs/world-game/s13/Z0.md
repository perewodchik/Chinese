# Z0 — the story bible and the place audit

**Why it helps the learner.** Nine chapters, four substories and ~20 books
will be written over several unattended nights. Without one source of truth
names drift, facts get invented, and chapters repeat the same lesson. The
bible makes every later line consistent and every fact checked — the
learner is told real things about real Beijing.

**What exists after Z0.**
- `docs/world-game/story.md` — the throughline (the 走马灯, ten places on
  its wheel, the nine memories, 兔儿爷's arc), a table per chapter (route in
  visit order, culture, books, 成语, spirit, cutscenes, substory episodes,
  side quests), the cast sheet, and conventions (ids for substories,
  cutscenes, books).
- `docs/world-game/facts.md` — every real-world claim with a source and a
  status (✓ / ~ / ? / ✗). Items still `?` must be checked by the task
  that first uses them.
- `scripts/world/coverage.ts` — prints, per map, the main steps, side
  quests, substory episodes and books pointing at it; `world:check` fails
  on maps off every main route only with `WORLD_COVERAGE=strict`.

**Decisions.**
- Chapter 5's spirit is **石猴**, hiding as a fourth monkey at 白云观;
  chapter 9's is **灶王爷**, who never ran away (he was in the kitchen) and
  lights on 除夕 — this makes "every figure lit but one" on 小军's arrival
  true and teaches 小年/除夕 through the plot.
- The lantern has **ten** places: nine spirits and one left blank by the
  grandfather, painted on the Wall as the family.
- 天官 stays the shrine's own story, not a lantern figure; one 元宵 night
  shows both endings.
- Substory episode order changed so each sits on a chapter route in
  order: 胡半仙 4→5→6→7→9, 老马/老牛 1→3→4→7→9, 甜甜 2→4→6→8→尾声,
  米沙 1→3→4→7→9.
- Stations and stops count as covered when their district is on a route.
- 百货大楼 is on the **west** side of 王府井大街 (the brief said east).

**Not done here.** No content changes (S1 changes 「灯笼是我奶奶的。」).
The `?` facts are left for their tasks rather than guessed now.
