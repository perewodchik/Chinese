# T3 — the temples

**Why.** The learner asked for more Chinese temples; chapter 5 (香火) is built
on them, and chapter 4's 天坛 needs its own 祈年殿 and 圜丘. Each map is the
real place's layout, simplified — the order you walk through, the thing it is
known for.

**New maps** (renders `review/t/<map>-day.png`):
- **雍和宫** — the courtyards (`yonghegong-front`): the main hall under yellow
  roofs with its studded door, side halls, smoking censers, rows of prayer
  wheels that turn as you pass, a bell, a stele, 蒲团; the hall's door leads
  into **万福阁** (`yonghegong-wanfuge`): the great **Maitreya** in gold on its
  lotus, lanterns, cushions, a 签筒 (sign: 18 m above, 8 m below, one
  sandalwood trunk — facts `yhg-maitreya`). The street's temple gate, a
  placeholder until now, opens into the courtyards.
- **孔庙** (`kongmiao`), next to 国子监 on the same street: 大成殿 and eighteen
  of the 198 进士 steles on their tortoises, with the numbers on a sign
  (facts `jinshi-steles`).
- **地坛** (`ditan`), across the road: the square altar 方泽坛 (天圆地方 on a
  sign); during 春节 the **庙会** appears — stalls, lanterns, a 冰糖葫芦 stand, a
  grill (props with `when: festival chunjie`), plain park otherwise.
- **天坛**: **祈年殿** (`qiniandian`) on its marble terrace with 丹陛桥 running
  south, and **圜丘** (`huanqiu`), three round tiers of white stone with the
  **天心石** at the centre (a sign now; S4's echo beat uses it). The park now
  leads north to 祈年殿 and south to 圜丘; the 回音壁 is as before.
- **白云观** (`baiyunguan`) by **木樨地** (new station on Line 1, with
  南礼士路): the glazed 牌楼, the gate with a stone monkey, another in a screen
  wall, the third at the foot of a stele (the three "who never meet"),
  **窝风桥** over the pool with the giant coin and its bell (打金钱眼), halls.
- **东岳庙** (`dongyuemiao`) by **朝阳门** (lines 2 and 6): the courtyard between
  galleries of "departments" (速报司 and the rest), steles, a censer — gentle,
  for 胡半仙's visit in chapter 5.

**Also.** Studded palace doors count as walkable (they are doors). New district
`xianghuo` (白云观 · 东岳庙; chapter 5 until S1 renumbers) and neighbourhoods
白云观 and 东岳庙; Line 1 on the diagram reaches 木樨地.
