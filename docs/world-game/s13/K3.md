# K3 — chapter openings and finales; the big moments as cutscenes

**Why.** A chapter should feel like it starts and ends; the four moments the
game is built around (the lantern breaking, the palace from 景山, the whisper
round the 回音壁, the dragon's eyes) should *happen on screen*, not only be
said.

**The template.** Opening = the chapter card (第X章 · name, English under)
over the district's landmark, a slow camera, one or two lines from the
people there, 兔儿爷's one English line on what we are here for. Finale =
the quest's last step `onDone` → a short cutscene where the chapter ends
(`finale: true`, ≤ 90 s): "第X章 · 完", 兔儿爷 on what's done and what's
next; the spirit's flight home and the memory at home come from K2.
Opening ids `<ch>-open`, finales `<ch>-finale` (story.md §4).

**Done here.**
- `ch1-open` — a new game, after the creator: the card over 南锣鼓巷 in the
  morning, you walk in from the station end, 赵爷爷 greets the new
  neighbour (「早！你是新来的吧？」 — you answer 「是。我住帽儿胡同。」),
  and home you go; it ends in your room, where the first morning starts. It
  is `auto` on arriving in your room (`on`) before the first morning, so
  saves already past it never see it.
- `lantern-breaks` — the lantern talk stops at 「灯笼坏了！」; the screen
  shakes, the lantern's picture breaks, seven spirits burst out and fly off
  in every direction, 王阿姨 is startled, 兔儿爷 sparkles into being; then
  his talk (`lantern-rabbit`, the old nodes c–f) starts by itself.
- `jingshan-view` — the first look from the 景山 view: the camera rises
  over the palace roofs and sweeps west to east (`before` on the view).
- `echo-wall` — before the whisper: the boy speaks into the wall, sparkles
  run round the curve to you (`before` on the listening talk, so the
  listening test itself stays a talk).
- `dragon-eyes` — you say 画龙点睛; two brush dots, the screen's eyes
  appear, lightning, the dragon rises and loops, sky lanterns, the card
  「画龙点睛」, fireworks; then the dragon speaks (`dragon-after`).
- `ch1-finale` — at 天安门东: 「天安门东到了！」, 「第一章 · 完」, 兔儿爷:
  one figure home, eight out there; next, 后海 and a fox.

**Links added.** A scene may name a cutscene `before` it (plays the first
time); a cutscene may name the `talk` that follows; `prop` steps change a
thing's picture; `on` sets where an `auto` cutscene starts. `world:check`
checks every link.

**Edge cases.** Nothing a player must *do* is inside a cutscene: every
spoken task stays a talk. The lantern's key line moved to `lantern-rabbit`,
so the lion now solves both `lantern/d` (old saves) and `lantern-rabbit/d`.

**Not done here.** The other chapters' openings and finales come with S2–S10
(the template is above). The dragon rising "over 太和殿" rises over the Nine
Dragon Screen instead, where the moment happens (no second map).
