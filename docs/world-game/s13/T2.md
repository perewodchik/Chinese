# T2 — 什刹海 · 银锭桥 · 恭王府 · 北海 白塔 · 景山

**Why.** Chapter 2 is "water and the hill": a learner should see two lakes
joined under a little bridge, climb to the White Dagoba, and stand among 景山's
five pavilions looking down on the palace — the way a visitor does.

**What changed** (renders in `review/t/`):
- **什刹海** (44 × 28): 后海 to the west, 前海 to the south-east, **银锭桥**
  over the neck between them (marble rails; a sign for 银锭观山, the view of
  the Western Hills). 烟袋斜街 comes in from the east on the north shore, with
  its tea, bar and 冰糖葫芦 fronts, willows and lanterns; the 什刹海 station
  on the north shore; the fisherman, the boatman and the boats. The south
  path from the bridge goes on to 北海's north gate. **恭王府**'s wall runs
  along 后海's south shore with its garden gate.
- **恭王府 garden** (new, 24 × 16): a pond, a pavilion, a rockery, a willow —
  the poetry club (X9 《红楼梦》) meets here now (its scenes moved with it).
- **琼华岛** (new, `beihai-baita`, 32 × 32): from 北海's north shore a boat
  pier takes you across; the pier, 永安寺's two green-roofed halls either side
  of the stair (smoking censers in the courtyard), the long stair up, and the
  **白塔** on its marble terrace (sign: built in 1651, a year before the Fifth
  Dalai Lama came to Beijing — facts `baita-1651`).
- **景山**: the four other pavilions on the ridge — 富览亭 and 辑芳亭 west of
  万春亭, 观妙亭 and 周赏亭 east — each with its name (facts
  `jingshan-pavilions`). The ch2 景山 view cutscene (K3) still plays at the top.

**Decided.** 恭王府's gate is a gap in its wall seen from the north (a 3/4
view shows a south-side building's back), so the garden is entered through a
lit gateway; its garden is named only "the garden of Prince Gong's Mansion"
until a source for 萃锦园 is checked.
