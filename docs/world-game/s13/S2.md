# S2 — chapter 2 水与山, deepened (design note)

**Why.** Chapter 2 was four steps (arrive, fisherman, 景山, fox), and most of the
lakes and the hill were scenery. Now it is 12 steps through every map of 后海 and
景山 · 北海. Each one is a small Chinese act: read a famous sign on a bridge, read a
book and answer from it, ask how to get somewhere (坐车还是坐船?), take a photo for a
friend on the phone, read five pavilion signs and pick the highest, travel home by
subway to borrow something, and answer the fox from the fable.

**What the player sees.** An opening pans over the lakes. On 银锭桥 the sign
银锭观山 starts a short cutscene of the Western Hills. The fisherman tells of the
fox and gives 《什刹海》. In 恭王府's garden the poetry-club girl asks where the
白塔 is (book p. 5) and how to get there (p. 6: by boat). At the island 王阿姨
phones for a photo of the 白塔. On 景山 the 太极 woman asks which pavilion is the
highest. The singer on 万春亭 sees the fox and gives 《狐假虎威》. The book says the
fox walks in front and the tiger behind, so the player goes home (line 6, one stop)
for 小明's roaring toy tiger. At the corner tower the fox asks where the tiger
walks (「在后面」), the toy roars, and the fox gives in: 狐假虎威. Then the finale
and 王阿姨's second memory: skating on 后海 with her grandfather, and the fox
copied from a book.

**Data.** New `ch2` steps bridge · lake-book · prince · beihai · baita · pavilions ·
fox-book · tiger, with the old ids kept in their order. `c2-*` scenes, cutscenes
`ch2-open`, `c2-yinding` (houhai), `memory-2`, `ch2-finale` (jingshan), item
`laohu` with a sprite, books `shichahai` and `hujiahuwei`, and facts
`shichahai-lakes`, `shichahai-skating`, `beihai-qionghua`, `hujiahuwei` (✓ now),
`hujiahuwei-school`.

**Edge cases.**
- Old saves at the fox step have no tiger, so the old `fox` scene still plays for
  them. It waits while a save is at any new earlier step. The new `c2-fox-tiger`
  needs the tiger and wins by priority.
- The singer waits for the pavilions step. The golden save `mid-ch2.json` (at
  `jingshan`, made with the content before S2) keeps its step.
- 小明 is at school 8:00–16:00, and the step says so.
- No money is needed: the island boat is the pier door.
- The fox now also solves chapter 1's 烟袋斜街 riddle.

**Rule changed.** `world:check` used to require a cutscene's map to be one of its
own district's maps. It no longer does: all content loads at once and the built
maps are checked anyway. This lets each chapter keep its memory cutscene
(on 王阿姨's yard) in its own folder, as S-howto says.

**Not done.** The 银锭桥 view has no real far-hills art; the cutscene pans west over
the water and 兔儿爷 names the hills.
