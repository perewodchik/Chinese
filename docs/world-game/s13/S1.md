# S1 — chapter 1 新家, deepened (design note)

**Why it helps the learner.** Chapter 1 was seven steps: meet, eat, the
lantern, a rumour, the lion, a card, the subway. Most of 鼓楼's streets were
scenery. Now the chapter has 18 steps, and each one asks for a different
everyday skill: reading a list back, buying at a shop and paying by phone,
saying how you want your hair cut, asking the way, answering a question
with something you read in a book, telling a child what you heard, and
reading a street sign. Each step is 2–6 minutes of play.

**What the player sees.** After breakfast 王阿姨 hands over her shopping list
and 20 元. The list is shown in her line and in the bag. At 李阿姨's shop the
player orders 「一瓶牛奶，一盒鸡蛋」 and pays on the phone, then brings them
home, and only then is shown the lantern. After the rumour, 张师傅 gives the
new neighbour a free haircut. 赵爷爷 shows the way to the 鼓楼 and gives
《石狮子》; at the towers a photographer asks which lion is the father. Page
3 of the book gives the answer, and the statues now show it: the east lion
has a ball, the west one a cub. The lion at night is unchanged. Then 老刘
gives 《晨钟暮鼓》, the new 鼓楼 attendant asks when the drum was beaten
(page 3: 晚上七点), and the evening drum is a cutscene: the drum, then the
bell answering from the north. The player photographs the 钟楼 for 小明,
tells him about it, and reads the sign to 烟袋斜街 (「听说后海那儿，也有一个小神……」,
pinned as a riddle for chapter 2). The card and the ride are unchanged.

**Data.** `ch1` steps list · shop · bring · haircut · lion-book · lions ·
drum-book · drum · bell · tell · yandai are new. Every old step id stays, in
the old order. New `c1-*` scenes and flags, the `c1-drum` cutscene, NPC
`gulou-ayi`, items `niunai` `jidan` `danzi` (with sprites), and books
`shishizi` and `chenzhong-mugu` (facts `lions-pair`, `lions-axis`,
`gengs-drum-bell`, `gulou-25-drums`, `zhonglou-bell`).

**Edge cases.**
- A save in the middle of chapter 1 keeps its step. New steps before it
  count as done, and those after it are ahead. The golden save
  `mid-ch1.json` (made at `lion` before S1) proves this.
- Old scenes that must wait for the new ones (`lantern`, `lion-night`) are
  gated with "not at step list/shop/bring" rather than a new flag. An old
  save is never at those steps, so it is never blocked.
- Money: 王阿姨's 20 元 covers the 15 元 list, the haircut is free, and the
  solver's spend-everything run still finishes.
- The drum question moves time on to 19:00 before the cutscene, so the
  player never has to wait for the right hour.

**Decided not to do.**
- A new drum-playing mechanic. The drum is heard, not played; the learning
  is in the question and the listening.
- Changing the hero's hair at the welcome haircut: the look action is not a
  content action, and the paid barber still offers a real cut.
- New suitcase art for the opening: it already works as the chapter's door.
