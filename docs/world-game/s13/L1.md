# L1 — the bike shop and the 修车摊

**Why.** A bike is *the* Beijing way to get about, and each part of owning
one is a real conversation: choosing, asking the price and the colour,
asking to try it (「我可以试试吗？」), paying, and later the repairman's
words (打气, 补胎). The measure word 辆 comes up every time.

**What the player sees.**
- **赵爷爷** on 南锣鼓巷 (from chapter 2) remembers riding his 永久 for twenty
  years, says you should have a bike, and gives **book 19 《自行车王国》**
  (level 3: the kingdom of bicycles, the three makes, the 二八大杠, the
  roadside repairmen, shared bikes today). Side quest `side-ziche`:
  buy a bike → show it to him.
- **自行车行** on 鼓楼东大街 (the shopfront T1 drew; "a bike shop", not a real
  one): 车行老板 stands outside with bikes on the pavement. His talk opens
  the **bike sheet** in the rack sheet's frame (W5): the three bikes as
  cards — each one drawn, its price, its colours as dots; the one looked at
  stands on the left with you beside it. 「我可以试试吗？」 → 「可以！骑一圈
  试试。」 and the bike rides up and down the stage, then 「怎么样？」.
  「我要这辆」 → 支付宝 (Y2). Once you own a bike the cards are the parts:
  车筐 30, 车锁 25, 后座 20. Too little money: the seller says so, 兔儿爷
  points at the jobs and at the recycler's cheaper bike.
- **Second-hand:** the recycler in 帽儿胡同 (mornings, once `side-ziche` is
  under way and you have no bike) has an old 永久, 250 元 asked, bargained
  (Y6) to about 150.
- **修车摊** (the stall T1 drew): 修车的老师傅 — 打气 free, a new bell 10 元
  (three bells: 叮, 叮当, 铃铃), 「这是什么？」 names the parts (车把, 车座,
  车轮, 车链); a flat tyre (L3) is 补胎 for 5 元. With no bike he points up
  the street to the shop.

**Data.** `core/bike.ts`: the models (`BIKES`: 永久 480 black/green, 凤凰 420
red/blue/white, 飞鸽 400 blue/green/black, the old 永久 250), the parts, the
save's `bike` (save **v17**), `bikeTurn` (the shop's talk) and `bikeHint`.
A node `bikes: { shop }` in content; content actions `bike`, `bike_part`,
`bike_fix`, `bike_bell`, `bike_home`; condition `{ bike: owned | riding |
flat | none }`. Art: `scripts/world/art/gen/bikes.ts` — each model and
colour from four sides, basket and rack overlays (props atlas `bike/…`).
The sheet: `ui/BikeSheet.tsx` (`Rider` draws you with a bike).

**Edge cases.** One bike: with one already, the shop sells parts only
(「你已经有自行车了！」). A part you have: 「你的车已经有了！」. The shop's
lines are HSK 1–2 plus the bike words (tested). The bike stands where you
bought it (on the pavement), not under you.

**Not done.** No bike catalogue as a content file — the four bikes and three
parts are tied to their drawings, so they live beside them in code. No
bigger choice of bells in the shop (the 修车摊 changes them). The bikes are
the game's own drawings; brands are names only (brands.md).
