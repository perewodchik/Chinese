# K2 — "you did it": step seals, quest seals, the spirit's flight home, memories

**Why.** Most steps used to finish silently. Understanding shows in what you
do, so the game should say, every time, "that was right" — small, the same
each time, never in the way.

**What the player sees.**
- **Step done:** a red seal 「✓」 lands at the top centre with the step's
  `past` line ("Woke the stone lion."), a wood block; 1.8 s. A fixed box
  over the world: it never moves anything and takes no tap (the talk goes
  on under it). The NextHop chip under the minimap glows once when it points
  somewhere new.
- **Quest done:** a bigger seal 「成」 with the quest's name and what it gave
  (🎁 糖葫芦 · 成语 马马虎虎 · ♥ 王阿姨), wood block + chime; 2.5 s. A chapter's
  finale cutscene is the last step's `onDone` (K3 writes them).
- **Spirit home:** a short template cutscene made for the map you are on:
  the spirit glows where it stood (or beside you), a bell, a flash, it flies
  off to the north-east, and the **走马灯 card** shows the wheel's ten
  places with its figure lighting up (3 s), then 兔儿爷's one line.
- **Memory:** next time you are home (the yard), 王阿姨 by the lantern tells
  one memory in 2–4 short lines — content cutscenes marked `auto` with the
  condition "this spirit is back". `memory-1` (the lion) is written here.
- ⚙ Sound → **Celebrations: Quiet / Full** (default Full). Quiet = seals
  only, silent; no flight home (story cutscenes still play).

**Data.** `core/celebrate.ts` (pure, tested): `sealsFor`, `rewardLine`,
`spiritsHome`, `spiritReturn` (the template), `LANTERN_FIGURES`,
`litFigures`, `autoCutscenes`. Cutscene steps gained `fly` (a spirit is not
bound to the ground) and the `lantern` overlay; cutscenes gained `auto`.
`settings.celebrate` is optional (no save bump: settings keep unknown keys).

**Edge cases.** Several seals at once show one after another. A synced save
from the other device earns its seals here too (it happened — a small
"welcome back"). Saves that already have spirits get each memory once, the
next time they are home.

**Not done.** No confetti on every line; "right answer in a talk" keeps the
existing ✓ on your line. Memories 2–9 come with their chapters (S2–S9).
