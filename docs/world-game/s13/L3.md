# L3 — small life with the bike (and L4, its checks)

**Why.** Owning a bike is a string of small real conversations — a flat
tyre, locking up, a neighbour noticing — never a chore, never a penalty.

**What the player sees.**
- **A flat tyre** at most once a week (never in your first three days with
  the bike, never while a timed step is under way), when you get on:
  兔儿爷 says 「车胎没气了！」 — you walk the bike beside you at walking pace
  (兔儿爷 out of the basket). The 修车摊 master: 「车胎坏了，要补胎。五块钱，
  好吗？」 → patched, and you ride again. The diary: 「我的自行车坏了，现在
  好了。」
- **The lock:** with a 车锁, getting off shows 🔒 beside 🚲; the first time
  兔儿爷 explains 锁车. Walk away without locking and nothing is ever
  stolen — 王阿姨 says, once, 「你的自行车没锁吧？……但是，自行车要锁！」.
- **Photos** with your bike (parked in the finder or ridden): 「我和我的自行车
  拍了照片！」 in the diary. **Friends notice** it once (the first friend you
  talk to: 王阿姨 「你买自行车了？真好！」).
- **side-bike** (the courier's shared bikes) now ends pointing on: once it
  is done and you have no bike, the courier says there is a shop up north.

**L4 checks.** `core/bike.test.ts`: the shop's talk and 💡 path, the save
(upgrade, merge, junk), the no-ride rules and `parkOnArrival` (station →
at its door with 「自行车不能进站」, a park gate, a shop with no sign), every
no-ride map real, every 骑车去 pair both ways with its sights and a walkable
arrival, the phone call, the flat tyre's week, the notice. `solver.test.ts`:
a broke player from the chapter-3 golden save earns a bike with the jobs; the
spendthrift still finishes everything. Renders: `review/l/bikes-*.png`
(every model and colour, four sides), `review/l/bike-parts.png`, and the
probe's screenshots `review/l/ui/` (Chromium, 375 / 768 / 1024, light and
dark: the shop, the test ride, riding, the 骑车去 row, the ride, arriving,
a park gate).
