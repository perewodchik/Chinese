# T1 — 鼓楼 · 南锣鼓巷 redone

**Why.** 「往北走」 should mean north. 南锣鼓巷 really runs north–south with
eight 胡同 off each side (蜈蚣巷, the centipede); the 钟楼 stands north of the
鼓楼 on the axis; 烟袋斜街 leads from the towers to 银锭桥. A learner who later
walks there will recognise it.

**What changed** (renders in `docs/world-game/review/t/`):
- **南锣鼓巷** (22 × 60) runs north–south: 鼓楼东大街 at the north end, the
  station's street 地安门东大街 at the south. Sixteen 胡同 open off it, each
  with its real name on a sign at its mouth (facts.md `nlgx-hutongs`); 帽儿胡同
  (home) is the fifth on the west side, three tiles wide, and leads west to
  your gate. The 理发店 and 早点铺 open on the west side, the 茶馆, 小卖部 and the
  文创 shop's window on the east, each a doorway in the wall with its sign.
  Red lanterns down both sides, 槐树, overhead wires, AC boxes, 小广告, the
  chess table, the 冰糖葫芦 stand, a shared-bike stand. People keep their
  ids; 赵爷爷 now stands at the mouth of 帽儿胡同.
- **钟鼓楼** (24 × 38): the 钟楼 at the north, the 鼓楼 at the south with its
  lion pair (the lion scenes keep `stone-lion`), the square between with
  ginkgos. Exits east to 鼓楼东大街 (by the Drum Tower) and 帽儿胡同, west to
  烟袋斜街.
- **烟袋斜街** (new, 40 × 16, district 后海): a lane that steps diagonally —
  as slanting as a pipe — with its pipe, tea, gift and snack shops and red
  lanterns, from the towers west to 后海 (T2 puts 银锭桥 there).
- **鼓楼东大街** (new, 48 × 16): a bike shop 自行车行 and a corner shop on the
  north side (L1 opens the bike shop), the 修车摊, a bus stop, a shared-bike
  rack, street lamps, ginkgos, a kiosk; 南锣鼓巷's mouth on the south side.
- **地安门东大街** (the station lane): 南锣鼓巷 now enters from the north; the
  street sign and place name say 地安门东大街 (part of 平安大街).
- The chapter-1 opening walks the new lane: in from the station end, 赵爷爷
  at 帽儿胡同's mouth, then west home.
- **Checks:** the map build now also fails when an edge's tiles are blocked
  or it lands on a blocked tile of the next map (it found the old prototype
  maps' exit); tests pin the new routes (鼓楼 → 烟袋斜街 → 后海; 鼓楼东大街 →
  南锣鼓巷 → the station).
