# T5 — map checks and renders

**Why.** Twenty-six maps were drawn or redrawn in T1–T4 without a browser;
the checks are what stands between the learner and a door into a wall.

**Checks now in place.**
- `build-maps` (fails the build): every door and every **edge** lands on an
  open tile of an existing map, and every tile of an edge exit is itself
  open (added in T1 — it found the prototypes' bad exit); a tile name used
  in two art files fails the art build (V3).
- `build-maps.test.ts`: the three north–south streets (南锣鼓巷, 王府井大街,
  前门大街) walk from their north end to their south end; every station hall
  is a station in `travel.ts`, and its way out is in that station's
  neighbourhood.
- `places.test.ts`: routes on foot pinned (鼓楼 → 烟袋斜街 → 后海 → 北海 → 景山;
  鼓楼东大街 → 南锣鼓巷 → the station; the palace in order; 神武门 one-way;
  前门大街 → 大栅栏 → 瑞蚨祥).
- `world:check`: every map is in a district's `district.json`, in a
  neighbourhood (`hoods.ts`) and has its thumbnail; the place audit
  (`coverage.ts`, strict only after S10) — 77 maps, 32 not yet on a main
  route (the new temples and streets wait for their chapters, S1–S10).

**Renders** (offline, `scripts/world/render-map.ts`, the view centred on
the map): `review/t/<map>-day.png` and `review/t/night/<map>-night.png` for
every new and reworked map, and `review/t/before/<map>-before.png` for the
ten reworked ones as they were before T1 (`RENDER_MAP_FILE` renders a map
file from git).

**Not done.** The browser map probe (`map-probe`) needs a dev server; the
offline renders stand in for it tonight.
