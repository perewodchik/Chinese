# Q1 — side quests you can't miss

**Why.** The learner asked for explicit side quests. 57 side quests are most
of the game's language practice, and §10's vague leads hid them. This
**reverses §10's "vague leads"** (logged).

**What the player sees.**
- **Over people:** a red paper tag 「!」 — the person the story needs now; a
  gold 「!」 — someone with a side quest you can start now; a small 「…」 —
  someone with the next step of a quest under way. Only when their scene
  would start *right now* (the same `sceneFor` the talk uses), so a mark
  never lies. The hint diamond steps aside where a quest mark stands. ⚙
  Controls → **Quest marks 「!」 On/Off** (on by default).
- **Journal → Side** (new view, between Now and Story): every side quest of
  the chapters reached, grouped by neighbourhood with yours first; under
  way, then "now", then the ones that wait with *when*: "mornings
  6:00–10:00", "at 中秋节", "on a rainy day", "from chapter 3", "once 王阿姨
  is a friend (♥3)", "later in the story". Each row: the giver's face, name,
  who and where, the first thing to do, what it gives. Tap → a sheet with
  the blurb, Who / First / When / Gives, the destination and the route with
  "Show on map". Journal → Now keeps one line: "3 side quests you could
  start now — Side ›". The red dot for new side quests moved to Side.
- **Maps:** the minimap and the neighbourhood plans put a dot in the marks'
  colours on each place; the metro map shows "3 new · 1 on" over each
  neighbourhood with side quests.
- **兔儿爷, once per neighbourhood per chapter** on arriving: "Someone here
  could use a hand — 李阿姨 at 小卖部."
- **Before a chapter's finale:** a small card "Before we go… (up to two open
  side quests of the chapter's streets) — still here, if you like. The story
  waits." with **Go on** (plays the finale) and **Not yet** (the finale
  plays on your next arrival anywhere). Never blocks.

**Data.** `core/sidequests.ts` (pure, tested): `sideQuests`, `whenText`,
`whoWhere`, `sceneMoves` / `sceneStartsSide` / `markOf` / `questMarks`,
`markedMaps`, `hoodCounts`, `arrivalNudge` (+ `nudgeKey` in `seen`),
`chapterHoods`, `openBeforeFinale`. Engine `setQuestMarks` draws 8×10 tags.
`settings.questMarks` optional (no save bump).

**Edge cases.** A start scene that can replay does not mark 「…」 (a quest
action counts only when it moves the quest forward). Quests of later
chapters are not listed (no spoilers, no wall of text). A quest with nobody
to ask is named by its title and place.
