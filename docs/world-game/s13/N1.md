# N1 — voices of the city (and N2, its checks)

**Why.** Listening needs hours, and real city sounds with real words are
hours for free: a station name said twice a ride, a street cry you start to
hear coming, the recycler's loudspeaker everyone in Beijing can imitate.

**What the player hears.**
- **In the train** (the ride sheet): the Chinese call as before
  (「下一站：王府井。可以换乘1号线。」, pre-rendered), then its English as
  Beijing's trains say it ("Next station: Wangfujing. You can transfer to
  Line 1."), 「请站稳扶好。」 as you pull out of the first station, and at a
  stop 「换乘1号线的乘客，请在本站下车。」 and 「开左侧门 / 开右侧门」 (which
  side is the game's own — facts `metro-doors`). All shown with the call.
- **Street voices** (`CITY_VOICES`): 「冰糖葫芦——」 on the busy streets (not
  in summer), 「磨剪子嘞——戗菜刀——」 in the 胡同 by day, the recycler's
  loudspeaker 「高价回收旧冰箱、旧彩电、旧手机……」 in the mornings (his hours),
  「热乎的烤红薯！」 in winter, 「包子，热乎的！」 at the 早点铺 6–10, the rider's
  「您的外卖到了！」 at lunch and supper, a scooter's 「请注意，倒车。」. One at a
  time, never over a talk, 40 s apart at least; each shows for 4 s as a
  caption at the right edge (who, the Chinese — tap a word for the word
  drawer — and EN). Spoken in the system voice (the voice worker is not
  reachable from here); ⚙ **City voices: on / off**.
- **Sounds without words** (`audio/city.ts`, synthesized like G1): chanting
  and the 木鱼 at 雍和宫, 白云观 and 东岳庙 by day; a 京剧 radio through a
  courtyard wall by day and 麻将 at night in the 胡同; 广场舞 music on the
  鼓楼 square and in the parks 18–21; 知了 on summer days; crows at a winter
  dusk; a few strings of firecrackers on 春节 and 元宵 evenings (not all
  night). The 鼓楼 drums stay at their show hours (V3).
- The city's sounds **duck while someone talks**, as the music does.

**Data.** `audio/mix.ts`: `mixFor(map, life, time, when)` → `sounds` and
`voices` (pure; `cityOf`, `CITY_VOICES`, the map sets). `audio/city.ts`: the
synthesis, context-agnostic. `ambient.ts`: a city gain under the street
volume, scheduled from the mix; the dance plays bar by bar. `core/ride.ts`:
`callEn`, `HOLD_ON`, `stopCalls`. Page: the mix refreshes every game hour;
a voice loop; `StreetCaption` (shared with L2's bell).

**N2 checks.** `mix.test.ts` for every rule (where, when, season, festival,
indoors), `ride.test.ts` for the calls; `scripts/world/sound-render.ts`
renders each sound offline with node-web-audio-api (not a dependency) and
fails if one is inaudible or more than 3 dB above G1's bicycle bell — the
street's reference level, set against the music. The table is in
`review/n/levels.txt`.

**Not done.** No CC0 recordings (synthesis was good enough to place and
test; a recording can replace one function in `city.ts`). No pre-rendered
voices for the street cries or the new train lines — the system voice reads
them until `build-voices` runs on the Mac. No 钟楼 bell as ambience (it is
not rung daily; the evening-drum cutscene has it).
