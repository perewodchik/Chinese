# K1 — the cutscene runner

**Why it helps the learner.** Big moments (the lantern breaking, a spirit
going home, 王阿姨 remembering) should *look* big, so the learner knows they
did something right; and a cutscene is listening with context — you see who
says what while the line is on screen. It must make sense with sound off.

**What the player sees.** Black bars slide in (a transform; nothing moves),
people walk and turn, the camera pans, sparkles / petals / sky lanterns /
fireworks, a chapter card in big 楷 with English under. Lines appear in a
read-only box in the game's pixel frame: speaker, the Chinese with every word
tappable (the word drawer opens; the cutscene waits for the tap on), 拼, and
the English under it; tap anywhere (or Space) to go on. ⏭ in the corner is
**held** for 0.6 s to skip (a ring fills); skipping snaps everything to how
the script ends. 兔儿爷's bubble, the top bar, the minimap and the joystick
are hidden while it runs; the clock stops.

**Data.** `content/world/<district>/cutscenes.json`, zod in `core/content.ts`,
types and the pure check in `core/cutscene.ts`: tiles on the map, walks on
walkable ground with a way there, actors on the cast / the map / spawned,
兔儿爷 English-only, ≤ 40 s (a finale ≤ 90 s, reading time counted). Every
Chinese line goes through the §5 word budget as a scene (`cutsceneScene`)
and to the voice builder. Triggers: the action `{ do: 'cutscene', id }`
(plays after the talk ends) and `onDone` on a quest step. `then: Action[]`
applies at the end (also after a skip). Save v13: `cutscenes: string[]`.
Journal → Story shows ▶ per watched cutscene of a chapter; a replay plays on
its map and brings you back, and gives nothing again.

**Edge cases.** A cutscene on another map travels there first and plays on
arrival (auto scenes wait). A talk that teleports and starts a cutscene:
the journey first. A synced save from the other device with a step done
queues that step's cutscene once (not if already watched there — merged).
Old saves: nothing watched; cutscenes of steps already passed never play by
themselves (they are only for steps done from now on).

**Decided not to do.** No hidden quiz in a cutscene; no per-object timers.
弹幕 and the seal are DOM (crisp CJK, same look as the rest of the overlay),
not canvas text. Sounds are synthesized (wood block, gong, bell, drum) —
N1 may replace them with better ones. `zoom` is a step over the usual zoom
(1 = as always), so pixels stay square on every screen.
