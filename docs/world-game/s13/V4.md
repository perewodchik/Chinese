# V4 — the spirits redrawn, woodcut pictures, a 4-frame walk

**Why.** The spirits are the characters the learner collects and remembers;
the fox was a white blob, the 年兽 a green one, the 麒麟 read as a bottle.
The lantern's figures should look like the painted figures they are.

**What exists now** (`scripts/world/art/gen/spirits.ts`; review
`docs/world-game/review/v/spirits.png`, `woodcuts.png`, `hero-walk.png`):
- **Every lantern figure redrawn, awake, with an idle** (32×32; the dragon
  48×32): the stone lion (collar and bell, ball under its paw; tail flicks,
  mouth opens), 九尾狐 (white, a red brow mark, nine gold-tipped tails that
  sway), 门神 (秦琼 fair-faced and 尉迟恭 dark-faced in gold armour, plumes
  flutter), 麒麟 (green scales, one horn, flames at mane and hooves), 貔貅
  (gold, winged, mouth open for a coin), 年兽 (a shaggy beast with a red lion-
  dance face; blinks and grins), 龙 (a long gold dragon, red fin and mane,
  whiskers, four clawed legs; its body ripples), and the new **石猴** (a small
  stone monkey with a peach), **灶王爷** (black hat, long white beard, red
  robe, the 笏 tablet) and **天官** (red robe, winged hat, the 天官赐福 scroll).
- **Woodcut pictures, 48×48**, in a new `woodcut` atlas: flat inks, a bold
  two-pixel line, warm paper in a red border with little cloud corners — the
  lantern's 年画 look. Also one for the lantern's tenth place, the family (a
  red gate with 福, a lit lantern, 王阿姨, 小军, you and 兔儿爷). 图鉴 shows the
  woodcuts in its grid and at the head of a spirit's sheet; the 走马灯 card
  shows them on its wheel.
- **The hero's 4-frame walk:** a passing frame `-3` for every direction,
  drawn by the W1 composer for every layer (so every outfit, hat and build
  has it), a pixel's dip mid-stride; the walk shows foot → passing → other
  foot → passing, and standing still the player breathes (the same dip now
  and then). The layer strips carry the frame too.
- Maps: the 角楼 fox and the 九龙壁 dragon use the new sprites; cutscene
  spirits keep their idle while they stand.

**Decided.** The passing frame dips rather than lifts: a lift pushed tall
hats off the top of the frame. Stone statues on the maps (the lion pair, the
bronze 麒麟, the 貔貅 in the bank, the painted door gods) stay statues — the
awake spirit is the new sprite.
