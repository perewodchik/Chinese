# S4 — chapter 4 回声, deepened (design note)

**Why.** Chapter 4 was four steps: arrive, the Echo Wall, 国子监, the 麒麟. 天坛's
great halls and the 孔庙 had nothing to do. Now it has 11 steps, and the two
temples are about listening and reading:
- answering a teacher's question in class (why is the roof blue?);
- saying a word on the 天心石 and hearing it come back;
- reading a 牌楼 and a stele of 进士 names;
- learning a saying of 孔子 in the place built for him.

**What the player sees.** Opening over 天坛. 小明's class is on a school trip; he
gives you 《天坛》. At the 祈年殿 his teacher asks the class why the roof is
blue (book p. 3: the colour of the sky). On the 圜丘 you stand on the 天心石,
say a word, and a short cutscene rings it back. Then the Echo Wall, as before,
whispers 国子监. On 国子监街 the 牌楼 says 国子监. The old scholar explains the
exams and gives 《科举》. In the 孔庙, 兔儿爷 asks how many names are on the
stones (p. 6: 五万多). 孔先生 gives you 「三人行，必有我师」 and a joke about
your rabbit. The 麒麟 at dusk, then 王阿姨's memory of studying to be a teacher,
and 兔儿爷's first small worry about what happens when the lantern is whole.

**Data.** New `ch4` steps school · roof · huanqiu · paifang · keju-book · names ·
kong, with the old ids kept in their order. `c4-*` scenes in tiantan and
yonghegong, NPC `xiaoming-laoshi`, and spawns for 小明 on tiantan-park and the
teacher on qiniandian, visible only during their steps. The saying
`三人行，必有我师` (yonghegong idioms). Books `tiantan` and `keju`. Cutscenes
`ch4-open`, `c4-huanqiu`, `memory-4`, `ch4-finale` (tiantan). Facts
`tiantan-round-square`, `tiantan-prayers`, `huanqiu-echo` (✓ now), `keju-exams`,
`guozijian-school`, `sanrenxing`.

**Edge cases.**
- The Echo Wall boy and the 麒麟 wait while a save is at any new earlier step.
- The golden save `mid-ch4.json` (at `echo`, made with the content before S4)
  keeps its step.
- No step costs money.

**Not done.**
- The 圜丘 echo is a chime and a sparkle, not your recorded voice.
- The 进士 stele shows no real names: the game does not invent names on a real
  monument.
