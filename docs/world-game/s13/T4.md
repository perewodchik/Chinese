# T4 — 王府井 · 前门/大栅栏 · 故宫, as they lie

**Why.** 王府井大街 and 前门大街 run north–south; the palace is a straight walk
north from 午门 to 神武门. A learner who follows 「往北走」 through the palace
walks the real axis.

**What changed** (renders `review/t/`):
- **王府井大街** (22 × 56, north–south, a walking street): the station at the
  south end (steps down); on the west side the bookshop, **百货大楼** (facts
  `wfj-baihuo-side`) with its windows either side of a double door, and the
  **小吃街** lane going west near the south end (facts `wfj-xiaochijie`); on
  the east side the pharmacy, the bank and 盛锡福's window with its assistant.
  Street lamps, benches, bins, trees, a kiosk. The shops' doors lead back to
  the right places; 百货大楼's two floors are unchanged.
- **前门大街** (22 × 56, north–south): the **箭楼** at the north end over a stone
  forecourt with the 前门 station; **老舍茶馆** (the old 戏园 map: tea and
  opera) and the roast-duck front on the east side; the 门神 gate (ch3's door
  gods) on the east wall; the **铛铛车** tram (a new prop) in the street;
  **大栅栏** going west.
- **大栅栏** (new, 40 × 16): the lane of 老字号 with **瑞蚨祥** and **内联升**
  (their doors moved here), tea and old shops, lanterns; the 大栅栏 granny
  lives here now (her scenes moved with her).
- **故宫 in order:** 午门 → 太和殿 → (new) **中和殿 · 保和殿 · 乾清宫** →
  御花园 → **神武门**, out to the moat and the 角楼. 神武门 is a way out only
  (`oneWay` on the door; the route finder respects it), so you enter at 午门
  as visitors do; 九龙壁 stays east of 太和殿.
