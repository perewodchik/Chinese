# L2 — riding

**Why.** The bike takes the friction out of getting between near places —
the thing a 20-minute learner feels most — and every rule of riding in
Beijing is taught by a sign you read: 禁止骑车, 请推行, 自行车不能进站.

**What the player sees.**
- **🚲 at the right edge** (above the talk box) once you own a bike — your
  bike's own picture. Not in the top bar as the brief said: at 375 px the
  bar has no room left and the place's name was squeezed out.
  Standing on the map where it is parked: tap to get on (or tap the bike
  itself). Riding: tap to get off — the bike stays where you are. Elsewhere:
  a small card says where it is, with **📞 Call 修车的老师傅**.
- **Riding** is twice as fast as walking (a shared bike: ×1.6). The bike is
  drawn under you from all four sides with its colour, basket and rack;
  with a 车筐, **兔儿爷 rides in the basket**.
- **🔔** while riding: the bell (the synthesized one); someone a few steps
  ahead turns and says 「慢点儿！」 — a street caption at the screen's edge
  (tap a word → the word drawer, EN → the meaning). Pure fun.
- **Where you can't ride.** Through a door: the bike stays at the doorstep.
  Into a park, the palace or a temple: you read 「禁止骑车 请推行」 and the
  bike waits at the gate. Into a station: 「自行车不能进站」 — it waits at the
  exit. (Rules: facts `parks-no-bikes`, `metro-no-bikes`.)
- **骑车去** — on the 🗺 metro map while you ride: the districts within about
  6 km (real distances, table below), nearest first, with the minutes. A
  ride is a short picture (6–15 s, Skip): you pedal while what you pass
  slides by — where you set off, **长安街** past 天安门 when you cross it, the
  lakes of 什刹海, where you are going — each named in Chinese. Game time =
  distance ÷ 15 km/h. You arrive on the district's main street, riding. The
  diary: 「我骑自行车去了后海。」.
- **The parked bike** is drawn on its map. Coming home by subway with the
  bike elsewhere, 兔儿爷 mentions it once. **Fetching it:** walk back, or the
  phone call — 「我的车在王府井大街，可以帮我送回来吗？」 — 10 元, at your gate
  in 帽儿胡同 the next morning.

**Distances** (straight line × 1.25 for streets, points from OpenStreetMap,
rounded; `core/bike.ts` `RIDE_PLACES`): 鼓楼 · 后海 · 北海 · 雍和宫 ·
王府井 · 前门 · 三里屯. Not offered: 颐和园, 奥林匹克公园, 长城 and 潘家园
(far — the train and the subway stay the way there), 天坛 and 天安门 (no
street to arrive on: the park and the square are no-ride).

**Data.** `bike.ts`: `noRide`, `NO_RIDE_MAPS`, `NO_RIDE_SIGNS`,
`RIDE_PLACES`, `rideOptions`, `rideSights`, `rideMs`, `callScene`; actions
`bike_on`, `bike_off`, `bike_ride`, `bike_home`. Engine: `setBike(look)`,
`ringBell()`. UI: `ui/BikeRide.tsx` (`BikeRide`, `BikeAway`, `RideChips`,
`StreetCaption`), the top bar's 🚲 and 🔔.

**Edge cases.** A save that says "riding" on a map you can't ride (an old
tab, a story teleport indoors) parks the bike where you came from. A flat
tyre (L3) cannot be ridden. With your own bike, the shared stands just say
you have one.

**Not done.** People do not step aside (they stand where the map puts them;
they turn and speak instead). No real 王府井 walking-street ban — the game's
王府井 and 前门 streets are ridden slowly like the rest, so the bike stays
useful.
