# V3 — animation everywhere

**Why.** A still world reads as a picture; Pokémon Black/White's streets
feel alive because *everything* idles. Life also carries culture: the old
man swinging his bird cage, the aunties' 广场舞, the incense smoke.

**What moves now** — all on **one clock** in the engine (a 90 ms tick; no
timer per thing, for the iPad), driven by the table `src/world/art/anims.ts`:
- **Tiles:** water in four frames with a glint (后海, 北海, the palace moat),
  the station escalator's stripes.
- **Props:** 早点铺 steam, 香炉 smoke, 烤串 smoke, 转经筒 turning **only while
  you are within three tiles** (they turn as you walk past), every tree and
  willow swaying a pixel (each in its own phase), flags (new: the national
  flag at the north end of 天安门 square, three at 国贸), a fountain (new:
  奥林匹克), traffic signals cycling, the fox and the dragon, rain puddles'
  ripples. Lanterns keep their flicker.
- **People:** a card may say what they do while they stand about (`idle`):
  a thing in their hand turns — 老刘 and the 后海 fisherman fan themselves,
  赵爷爷 swings his bird cage, the chess grandpa lifts a piece, the teahouse
  guest reads, the couriers check their phones, the 大栅栏 granny knits, the
  天坛 grannies kick a 毽子 or spin a 空竹; the 景山 auntie does 太极 (slow
  turns and a rise), the dancing auntie steps to the beat. They no longer
  glance about at random while they do it.
- **Seasons on the map (out of doors):** gold ginkgo leaves drift in autumn;
  in winter snow lies on ridges, eaves and wall tops, and crows cross the
  sky at dusk; 柳絮 floats in spring; in the rain, puddles with ripples; in
  the morning a pale mist breathes over maps with water.
- **The 鼓楼 drum shows** at about 9:30, 10:30, 11:30, 13:30, 14:30, 15:30 and
  16:30 (facts.md): standing on the square, you hear six beats.
- **Depth:** 兔儿爷 is sorted by his feet every frame, so behind you he is
  behind you even while he floats (the "rabbit over the hero's head" bug).

**Checks.** `art.test.ts`: every frame the table names is in the built
atlases; the kit has twelve roof tiles per glaze; tile names are unique (the
art build now fails on a tile name in two files — it caught the kit's roof
tiles shadowing the old ones, renamed `pitch-*`).

**Not done here.** Hero walk/run/breathing frames (V4), pedalling (L2), a sun
shaft in temple halls (no hall interiors until T3), a lantern sway (rotating
pixel art smears — flicker stays). **Frame time on the iPad profile not
measured** (needs the dev server): the whole clock is one loop over the
things on the map; particles are sparse (one leaf every 0.7 s).
