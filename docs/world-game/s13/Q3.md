# Q3 — "last time" recap

**Why.** After a day away a learner forgets both the plot and the words. A
ten-second reminder of each makes the next twenty minutes count.

**What the player sees.** Opening the game twelve real hours or more after
the save last changed: a small card over the world (not a cutscene), with
兔儿爷's portrait and 上次 · Last time:
- "Last time: met 王阿姨 in the courtyard." (the story's last entry)
- "Next: Have breakfast at the 早点铺…" (what 兔儿爷's "What now?" says)
- "And a few words from last time:" — up to five word chips (hanzi and
  pinyin; tap → the word drawer) each with 🔊.
One button: **Let's go** (Esc closes too). It waits for the creator, a
cutscene or a talk to finish, and shows once. `?recap=1` shows it at once.

**Data.** Save **v14**: `lastWords` — each finished talk with a person adds
its situation words and its hints' key words (at most four a talk); eight
kept, newest last; merge takes the later device's. `core/recap.ts` (pure,
tested): `wordsOfScene`, `recapOf`, `isAway`; `rememberWords` in save.ts.

**Edge cases.** A new game has nothing to recall: no card. A save with no
words yet shows the plot only. The twelve hours are wall-clock (`updatedAt`),
so a second device that just synced shows no card.

**Not done.** No quiz on the words — help is free and nothing is a test; the
chips are there to hear and to open.
