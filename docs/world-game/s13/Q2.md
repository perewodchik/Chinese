# Q2 — waiting for a day or a festival

**Why.** The story now reaches 春节 and 元宵, and many quests wait for a
morning or a festival. Walking in circles to wait is dead time for a
20-minute learner; saying 「睡到中秋节」 is not.

**What the player sees.**
- **The bed** (your room): when the step you follow — or the story's — can
  only happen on a later day (a festival, a season, the weather, "not again
  today"), the bed asks 「睡觉吗？还是睡到中秋节？」. Saying 睡觉 sleeps to
  7:00 as before; saying 睡到… (or the festival's name) sleeps till 7:00 on
  that day. Not a festival → the date: 「睡到9月24号」. Only past tomorrow
  (tomorrow is what 睡觉 does anyway). Nothing to wait for → the bed as it
  always was.
- **The diary** gets one line for each whole day slept through:
  「这一天我在家休息。」 — no holes.
- **Waiting for an hour:** when the step you follow waits for a time of day,
  the next-hop chip says "· after 17:00"; standing where it happens, the chip
  is "⏳ after 17:00" with **Wait here** — only where you can sit (a
  teahouse, a stool, a table, a barber's chair, a park or the 鼓楼 square).
  Waiting uses the existing `wait` (the clock jumps; 兔儿爷 nods off).

**Data.** `core/rest.ts` (pure, tested): `sleepTarget`, `bedScene` (the bed's
talk with the 睡到 answer first, the plain 睡觉 still there), `stepWait`,
`canWaitHere`. `{ do: 'sleep', until: day }`; diary code `q`.

**Edge cases.** A step waiting for both a festival and an hour: the bed
wakes you on the day, the chip then says the hour. A target already past
sleeps as usual. The calendar loops every 52 days, so a festival just gone
is found next year.

**Not done.** No "sleep till" from anywhere but the bed (home is where you
sleep); no skipping hours on the street without a seat.
