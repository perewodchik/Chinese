What a day looks like

  • 读 ReadTexts written at your level, with pinyin above the characters, the words you are learning marked, and any word a tap away.
  • 卡 CardsA fixed few a day, the ones that are slipping first. Never a pile of debts.
  • 说 SpeakSay a line from your text and hear native recordings; with a SpeechSuper key of your own, see which initials, finals and tones were off.
  • 写 Copy outWrite the text into your exercise book a word at a time, with the strokes beside it.
  • 玩 PlaySmall games for the words of the week, and a walk through Beijing in Chinese.
